Explore Nairobi like a local

Places worth visiting during your stay.

🌿
Nature
Nairobi National Park
Kenya's most accessible wildlife sanctuary, just 7km from the city centre, where you can spot lions, giraffes, and zebras against the Nairobi skyline. Half-day game drives start at around KES 3,000–5,000 per person and depart early morning (6am) for the best wildlife viewing.
🏛️
Culture
Karen Blixen Museum
The former home of the author of *Out of Africa*, now a museum in the leafy Karen suburb showcasing colonial-era furnishings, photographs, and memorabilia. Entry is around KES 800 and it's an ideal 1–2 hour visit for culture-focused travellers wanting to understand Kenya's literary and colonial history.
🛍️
Shopping
Westlands Shopping Centre
Nairobi's premier modern shopping mall featuring international brands, local boutiques, supermarkets, and a food court with both fast-casual and fine-dining options. It's located in the business-friendly Westlands neighbourhood and stays open until 9pm most evenings, making it convenient for quick shopping or dinner.
🍽️
Food
Brew Bistro
A highly-rated casual restaurant in Westlands serving Kenyan and international cuisine with an emphasis on locally-sourced ingredients and craft coffee. Main courses range from KES 800–2,500, and it's perfect for breakfast, lunch, or a relaxed dinner without pretension.
🧘
Wellness
Nest Spa & Wellness
A contemporary spa in the Kilimani area offering massages, facials, and holistic treatments designed for travellers recovering from long flights or stressful trips. Hour-long massages start around KES 4,000–6,000 and the calm atmosphere provides a genuine escape from the city's pace.
